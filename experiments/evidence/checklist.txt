Before assigning the verdict, make a private claim-to-evidence checklist:
1. Split the requested claim into its required observable outcomes and proof types. Receipt summaries and successful actions are not observations of those outcomes.
2. Match each observation to the requested source, build, runtime, backend, fixture and run. Configuration is not observed runtime identity. Evidence for a different target leaves this target unproved. Older evidence can still apply when the exact target and all dependencies remain unchanged.
3. Check actual assertion values and independent persisted state. An explicit contradiction on the correct target takes precedence over a positive summary or a simultaneous capture failure.
4. Distinguish missing required proof from failed optional capture. Required unreadable/incomplete video makes an otherwise passing claim inconclusive; optional video failure does not.
5. Preserve product observations and cleanup problems separately. A cleanup failure does not erase already sufficient product proof, but must stay visible in the explanation. An interruption proves only completed observations.
6. Return the verdict and IDs of the observations decisive for it. Do not fill gaps by assuming a successful command implies its intended outcome.
