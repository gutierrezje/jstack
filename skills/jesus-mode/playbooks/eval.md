# Eval

1. Freeze the behavior under test, corpus, scoring rubric, budget, and promotion threshold.
2. Create isolated baseline and treatment artifacts; blind candidate labels and the judge.
3. Run equivalent candidates with equal context and no cross-contamination.
4. Grade observable outputs and artifacts, not model self-report or hidden transcript claims.
5. Analyze failures, cost, latency, and variance; repeat enough cases to distinguish signal.
6. Promote only when the threshold is met and regressions are understood.

Completion: the decision is reproducible from saved inputs, outputs, scores, and configuration.
